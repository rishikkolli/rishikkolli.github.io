The route, 2027 → 2032

Tentative. Starts May 2027 and ends before the 30th birthday on Sep 3, 2032. Trips are grouped by season and region to save PTO and flights. The build runs from climbs to marathons, then 100-milers, then Badwater.

Months are each race's usual window. Check every year's calendar and sign up early for lotteries and sell-outs (Leadville, Cocodona, MdS, Comrades). Badwater needs three finished 100-milers, one within the previous 18 months: Leadville '28, Cocodona '29 and The Border '31 cover that.
